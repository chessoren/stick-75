Frequently asked

How do I cancel the weekly subscription?
Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Stick. The 75-Day Pass is a one-time purchase and never renews.

How do I restore a purchase?
Paywall › Restore, or Me › Subscription › Restore purchases.

How do I delete my voice clone or all my data?
Me › My voice › Delete my voice clone, or Me › Account › Delete everything. The voice clone is deleted at our voice provider too.

Stick doesn't ring when I open TikTok.
Set up the Shortcuts automation: Me › Interception, and follow the four steps.
